# Code of Conduct

We want everyone taking part in these projects to feel respected and welcome.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully.
- Do not harass, insult, discriminate against or demean anyone, and do not share other people's private information.
- Keep discussions on topic and professional.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any public place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these standards. To report a problem, email **info@zenxsys.com** with the subject "Code of Conduct". Reports are handled privately and with care.
